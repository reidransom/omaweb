  <h2 id="meetups-guidance-title">Run your own Omarchy meetup</h2>
  <p>Meetups are community-run. They can be about Omarchy, Linux, open source, programming, customization, or nearby hacker culture.</p>
  <ol>
    <li>Make it open to everyone. Capacity limits are practical; invitation-only events presented as official Omarchy gatherings are not.</li>
    <li>Speak for yourself. An Omarchy meetup is a community event, not an endorsement by Omarchy or the Omacom Foundation.</li>
    <li>Anyone can organize in any city. Nobody owns a place.</li>
    <li>Prefer free events. Charging for a venue, food, or actual event costs is fine; using the name as an events business is not.</li>
    <li>Local sponsors may provide space, food, or drinks when disclosed. Keep the gathering from becoming a sales pitch.</li>
    <li>Treat people like civilized adults. Organizers decide who must leave their event.</li>
    <li>Use the name and logo responsibly. “Omarchy Copenhagen” is fine; do not imply that you are Omarchy, the Foundation, or an authorized representative.</li>
    <li><a href="https://luma.com/omarchy" rel="noreferrer">Submit the event to Luma</a> so it can appear on the shared calendar.</li>
  </ol>
