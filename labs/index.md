<section class="prose" aria-labelledby="labs-question-title">
  <h2 id="labs-question-title">Some ideas need a bench before they need a name.</h2>
  <p>Labs is where Omarchy can make room for public experiments without pretending they are products. An interface concept, an agent workflow, or an unfamiliar device can be worth examining before anyone decides whether it belongs in the everyday system.</p>
  <p>Curiosity is useful when it stays honest about what it has not settled.</p>
</section>

<section class="prose" aria-labelledby="labs-experiments-title">
  <h2 id="labs-experiments-title">What could a computer become when the usual screen is not the only answer?</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Which interface ideas make a complex system easier to shape without making it mysterious?</li>
    <li>How might agent workflows give people more leverage while keeping their choices inspectable?</li>
    <li>What do alternative devices, voice, or ambient computing reveal about the parts of a computer that matter most?</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="prose" aria-labelledby="labs-air-title">
  <h2 id="labs-air-title">Experiments need more than engineering input.</h2>
  <p>Artists in Residence brings artists into the wider Omarchy project. Labs can stay adjacent to that work: a place to notice strange directions, share an experiment, and let a good question remain open.</p>
