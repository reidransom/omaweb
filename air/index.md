<section class="prose" aria-labelledby="air-purpose-title">
  <h2 id="air-purpose-title">Give artists room to work</h2>
  <p>Artists in Residence is a six-month Omacom Foundation residency for people working on themes, plugins, and other work that makes Omarchy beautiful. Residents choose the work; there is no prescribed roadmap or ticket queue.</p>
</section>

<section class="prose" aria-labelledby="air-residents-title">
  <h2 id="air-residents-title">Current residents</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><a href="https://github.com/HANCORE-linux" rel="noreferrer">HANCORE</a> has contributed Solitude and Last Horizon, which ship with Omarchy, alongside many additional themes.</li>
    <li><a href="https://github.com/OldJobobo" rel="noreferrer">OldJobobo</a> made the Lumon, Miasma, and Retro 82 themes that ship with Omarchy, as well as additional themes.</li>
    <li><a href="https://github.com/tahayvr" rel="noreferrer">Taha</a> drew the Omarchy logo and created Omarchist, a graphical theme-making tool.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="prose" aria-labelledby="air-terms-title">
  <h2 id="air-terms-title">What the residency provides</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>$2,500 per month for the six-month residency.</li>
    <li>A token account for agent use.</li>
    <li>Permanent recognition as AIR alumni after the residency.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>There are up to five seats at one time.</p>
</section>
