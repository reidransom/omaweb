# Publish cleared Desktop and Workstations proof media

Status: ready-for-agent

## Problem

Desktop has no local product media, and Workstations has no cleared community photographs. Both routes therefore lack the current proof required by the proposal.

## Scope

- Research rights-clear Desktop media and a curated subset of current community workstation photographs; localize only candidates whose source and publication permission are clear.
- Record sources, permissions, ownership, checksums, dimensions, responsive derivatives, and useful alt text in the asset manifest.
- Render Desktop media as responsive local proof and Workstations as the bespoke gallery composition.
- Do not fabricate a screenshot, desk, photo, testimonial, rights clearance, or product claim; an allowed illustrative placeholder is not factual proof.

## Media-sourcing decision

Research rights-clear candidates by web search before asking for new media. When no suitable cleared candidate exists, a local OpenAI image-model placeholder may fill the layout, but it must be explicitly illustrative and must not be represented as a real workstation, person, product proof, News event, or other factual evidence.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Desktop includes at least one cleared, manifested, responsive local product-media surface with truthful alt text.
- Workstations shows a populated gallery with cleared community photographs where available; otherwise it uses clearly identified illustrative placeholders rather than claiming current community proof.
- Every rendered proof image is local, cleared, dimensioned, lazy below the fold, and selected through viewport-appropriate sources.
- If approved Workstations source photographs cannot be cleared, record the missing clearance and present only the explicitly illustrative placeholder state; never claim it is a real workstation.
