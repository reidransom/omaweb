# Ground Labs in verified experiments

Status: ready-for-agent

## Problem

Labs presents abstract prompts without a named experiment, actor, mechanism, or observed result. That weakens the site’s factual exploratory-status boundary and violates the project’s concrete-copy standard.

## Scope

- Replace generic Labs claims with verified, current experiment descriptions and their relationship to Artists in Residence where facts exist.
- Keep the literal Exploratory label in the Labs hero, homepage card/section, and group card.
- Describe experiments as experiments, never as shipping products, commitments, availability, specifications, compatibility, certification, dates, prices, or roadmap items.
- If no concrete verified experiment is available, reduce the page to the factual scope statement rather than inventing one.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Labs names at least one verified concrete experiment, actor, or mechanism, or contains only a restrained factual statement of the project’s exploratory remit.
- Copy uses plain active language and avoids unsupported generalities.
- Labs retains its distinct AIR-adjacent role without claiming a product release.
- Browser-visible copy continues to meet the exploratory-status contract across its route and cards.
